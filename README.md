## Overview


### Problem

AI and automation are transforming payroll and workforce administration, reducing the reliance on repetitive clerical tasks. Payroll and Timekeeping Clerks often lack visibility into emerging career opportunities, evolving skill demands, and effective reskilling pathways. As a result, many workers risk being unprepared for future workforce changes.

### Solution

FuturePath AI helps Payroll and Timekeeping Clerks prepare for workforce changes caused by AI and automation. The platform analyses current skills, identifies emerging skill trends, recommends future career opportunities, and generates personalised reskilling pathways. By providing explainable recommendations and scenario-based insights, users can make more informed career transition decisions.

### Key Features

- **Skills Profile Intake** — Collects user current skills and professional background.
- **AI-Trends Signal Layer** — Tracks emerging and declining skills affected by AI-driven workforce changes.
- **Future-Role Matching** — Recommends future-ready career opportunities based on skill alignment.
- **Skill Gap Analysis** - Identifies skills required to transition into recommended roles.
- **Personalised Reskilling Pathway** - Generates structured learning recommendations to support career transitions.
- **SDG Mapping** - Shows how careers and skills contribute to selected Sustainable Development Goals.
- **Handling Uncertainty** - Compare career recommendations under different AI disruption scenarios.
